# Product Vision

**Version:** 0.1
**Project:** Enclosure Lab

---

# Vision Statement

Enclosure Lab is an intelligent loudspeaker engineering workbench that guides users from driver selection to a finished, validated loudspeaker enclosure through explainable engineering, optimization, and practical cabinet design.

Unlike traditional enclosure calculators, Enclosure Lab does not begin with a box type—it begins with the user's design goals and recommends the most appropriate solution while explaining every engineering decision.

---

# Mission Statement

To make professional loudspeaker enclosure design accessible to everyone by combining engineering principles, simulation, optimization, and education into a single platform.

Whether the user is designing their first bookshelf speaker or a professional line array, Enclosure Lab should provide clear recommendations, transparent reasoning, and build-ready designs.

---

# Our Philosophy

Enclosure Lab is built around five principles.

## 1. Explain Every Recommendation

Every recommendation must answer:

* Why was this chosen?
* What alternatives were considered?
* What tradeoffs exist?
* How confident is the recommendation?

---

## 2. Design for Reality

Every enclosure should be practical to build.

Predictions should account for:

* Driver displacement
* Port displacement
* Bracing
* Material thickness
* Construction tolerances

Simulation should reflect real-world construction.

---

## 3. Validate Everything

Predictions should be compared against measured performance whenever possible.

Measurements from REW and other supported tools should refine future designs and improve confidence.

---

## 4. Teach While Designing

The software should help users understand loudspeaker engineering.

Advanced concepts should be explained in plain language while still exposing the underlying calculations for experienced designers.

---

## 5. Build Once, Build Right

Reduce unnecessary prototype iterations by helping users make informed engineering decisions before cutting material.

---

# What Makes Enclosure Lab Different?

Traditional speaker software typically asks:

> "What enclosure would you like to design?"

Enclosure Lab asks:

> "What are you trying to build?"

From that answer, the software evaluates suitable enclosure types, explains the reasoning behind each recommendation, and helps the user arrive at the best overall design.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Enclosure Lab will become a complete loudspeaker development platform.

Driver → Analysis → Recommendation → Optimization → Cabinet Design → Build → Measurement → Validation → Refinement

The goal is not simply to calculate enclosure volumes.

The goal is to become the engineering workbench that loudspeaker designers use throughout the entire design process.

---

# Project Motto

**Every recommendation should be explainable. Every prediction should be verifiable. Every design should be buildable.**
