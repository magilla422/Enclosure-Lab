# Product Vision

**Project:** Enclosure Lab
**Document Version:** 0.1
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-12

---

"Enclosure Lab is not designed to calculate loudspeaker enclosures. It is designed to engineer complete loudspeaker systems."

---

# Vision Statement

Enclosure Lab is an intelligent loudspeaker engineering workbench that guides users from driver selection to a finished, validated loudspeaker enclosure through explainable engineering, optimization, and practical cabinet design.

Unlike traditional enclosure calculators, Enclosure Lab does not begin by asking the user to choose an enclosure type. Instead, it begins by understanding the user's goals and recommends the most appropriate solution while clearly explaining every engineering decision.

---

# Mission Statement

Our mission is to make professional loudspeaker enclosure design accessible to everyone by combining engineering principles, simulation, optimization, education, and practical design tools into a single application.

Whether the user is building their first bookshelf speaker or developing a professional line array, Enclosure Lab should provide understandable recommendations, transparent engineering, and buildable results.

---

# The Problem

Current loudspeaker design software often assumes the user already understands enclosure theory and knows which enclosure type to design.

This creates several challenges:

* Beginners are overwhelmed by technical terminology.
* Experienced users must switch between multiple software packages.
* Recommendations are often presented without explanation.
* Predicted performance is rarely connected to real-world measurements.
* Cabinet design and acoustic simulation are separated into different workflows.

Enclosure Lab is designed to bridge these gaps.

---

# Our Solution

Enclosure Lab combines enclosure recommendation, engineering analysis, optimization, cabinet design, and measurement validation into a single workflow.

Instead of asking:

> "What enclosure do you want to build?"

Enclosure Lab asks:

> "What are you trying to accomplish?"

The software then evaluates the available enclosure types, explains the engineering tradeoffs, and guides the user toward the most suitable solution.

---

# Core Principles

## Explain Every Recommendation

Every recommendation must answer:

* Why was this enclosure selected?
* Why were the alternatives ranked lower?
* What tradeoffs exist?
* How confident is the recommendation?

Users should understand the engineering, not simply accept a result.

---

## Design for Reality

Every enclosure generated by Enclosure Lab should be practical to build.

Calculations should account for:

* Driver displacement
* Port displacement
* Bracing
* Material thickness
* Construction tolerances

Simulation should represent the enclosure that will actually be built.

---

## Validate Every Prediction

Whenever measurements are available, predicted performance should be compared against measured performance.

The software should encourage verification using tools such as REW and help users understand differences between prediction and reality.

---

## Teach While Designing

Enclosure Lab should educate users throughout the design process.

Beginners should be able to understand recommendations through plain-language explanations while advanced users retain access to the underlying engineering calculations.

---

## Build Once, Build Right

The objective is to reduce unnecessary prototype iterations by helping users make informed engineering decisions before cutting material.

---

# Target Users

Enclosure Lab is intended for:

* DIY loudspeaker builders
* Home audio enthusiasts
* Car audio designers
* PA system designers
* Loudspeaker manufacturers
* Educational institutions
* Makerspaces
* Acoustical engineers

---

# What Makes Enclosure Lab Different

Traditional enclosure software focuses primarily on simulation.

Enclosure Lab focuses on decision making.

The software will recommend enclosure types, explain its reasoning, optimize designs around user goals, generate build-ready cabinets, and validate predictions with real-world measurements.

It becomes an engineering assistant rather than simply an enclosure calculator.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Enclosure Lab will become a complete loudspeaker development platform.

Driver → Analysis → Recommendation → Optimization → Cabinet Design → Build → Measurement → Validation → Refinement

Every stage of loudspeaker development should exist within a single project, allowing users to manage the complete engineering process from concept to finished speaker.
